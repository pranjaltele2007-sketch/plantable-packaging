Entrepreneurship Milestone 1 Research

The Packaging Dilemma Facing Conscious Brands

You spend months formulating clean, organic, chemical-free products. But when customers open your package, they are greeted by bubble wrap, plastic tape, and disposable boxes destined for landfills.

The Problem with Traditional Packaging

  • Contradicts Brand Values: 8 out of 10 interviewed organic brand founders revealed their customers actively complain about single-use packaging waste.
  • The "Compostable" Myth: Most bioplastics require 60°C industrial composting plants that everyday consumers don't have access to.
  • Exorbitant Global Imports: International seed paper suppliers charge heavy import duties and ₹110+ per box with 45-day lead times.

The Plantable Packaging Solution

  • Dual-Life Transformation: Functions as sturdy transit protection, then transforms into fresh Tulsi, Mint, or Marigolds on the customer’s windowsill.
  • True Backyard Compostability: Breaks down naturally in household plant pots or backyard soil within days without chemicals.
  • Localized Indian Supply Chain: Handcrafted and die-cut in Pune, slashing shipping lead times to 5–7 days and unit costs by up to 60%.
